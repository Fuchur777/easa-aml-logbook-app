## Full description (4000 max)

```
AMlog is a personal maintenance logbook and certificate-of-release-to-service generator for holders of an EASA Part-66 aircraft maintenance licence. It is built for independent certifying staff keeping their own records — not a fleet system, not a CAMO tool, and not something that reports to anyone.

RECENCY, WORKED OUT AS YOU LOG

Recency is assessed separately for each subcategory you hold, over a rolling 24-month window. Every route to staying current is shown side by side, so you can see which one you are living on and how close the others are:

- 100 days of maintenance experience in the window, or 50 where your competent authority has agreed a reduction
- Half the Appendix II tasks that apply to the subcategory, with at least one completion in every section
- Annual inspections — shown greyed out and not counted while it remains a proposal (NPA 2025-12)
- Within 24 months of initial certification, current by default

Amber then red warnings show when a licence expiry or a recency lapse is approaching, with the notice periods set by you.

CERTIFICATES

Generate a CRS against a work entry with the content ML.A.801 requires, including limitations, deferred items and who assisted. Sign it on the device with a hardware-backed key released by your fingerprint, or print it, sign by hand, and attach a photograph of the signed original. Issued certificates are immutable, numbered to a template you define, and collected in a library you can filter, search and export.

YOUR RECORDS

Aircraft, documents and contacts, each reusable across work entries. Documents can be filtered by category and by the aircraft they were used on. Photographs attach to work entries as evidence of what was done.

BACKUP, IF YOU WANT IT

Optionally connect your own Google Drive. Certificates, photos, documents and full-device backups go to that account, and a backup can be restored onto a new phone. Everything in the app works offline without it.

PRIVACY

AMlog has no backend and the developer never receives your data. Your records are held on this device, and copied only into your own Google account — by Android's own app backup, and by Drive sync if you switch it on. Nothing is sent anywhere else. There is no analytics and no advertising.

IMPORTANT

AMlog does not determine your privileges or your recency status. It records what you enter and shows a calculation from it, and it cannot see work logged on paper or before you installed it — so a subcategory shown as not current may simply be incompletely recorded. You remain solely responsible for verifying compliance with your competent authority's requirements before relying on anything the app displays.

Certificates signed on the device use a hardware-backed key with no third-party trust provider behind it. Your competent authority, not this app, is the trust anchor for that signature.

AMlog is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced by EASA or any national aviation authority.
```
